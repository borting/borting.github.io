LCI (Location Configuration Information) report was introduced in 802.11k to provide geodetic location.
Locatin Civic report was introduced in 802.11v to provide civic location.

Geodetic location describes location mathematically, in terms of the WGS84 (World Geodetic System 1984) coordinate reference system.
Civic location describes location in terms of addresses and other related constructs.
