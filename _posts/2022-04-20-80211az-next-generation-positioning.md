# Revolution

發展順序:
1. RSSI-based positioning
2. 802.11mc Fine Timing Measurement (FTM): 借用 802.11v Timing Measurement 引入的 Time of Flight 技術實現
3. 802.11az Next Generation Positioning (NGP): 強化 FTM 的隱蔽性與準確度
