New features introduced in 802.11ax.

-- High density
* OFDMA
	* UORA (UL OFDMA-based Random Access)
	* Operating Mode Indication (OMI)
	* tone
	* RU
		* HE SIG-B
* DL MU-MIMO
* Trigger frame

-- Throughput
* 1024-QAM

-- Efficiency
* Dynamic fragmentation
* Multi-TID aggregation

-- Spatial Reuse
* Type
	* OBSS PD(packet detection)-based spatial reuse
	* SRP-based spatial reuse
* BSS Coloring
* Dueling NAV

-- Power saving
* TWT

-- PHY
* GI --> 0.8, 1.6, 3.2 us
* LTF
* PPDU format
	* HE SU PPDU
	* HE ER (extended range) SU PPDU
	* HE MU PPDU
	* HE TB PPDU
* Midamble
* DCM (Dual Carrier Modulation)
* Padding
